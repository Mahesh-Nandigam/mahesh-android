# 🔒 Privacy Policy for Mahesh-

**Effective Date:** October 2026

**Mahesh-** ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy.

### 1. Audio and Microphone Processing
* **Local Offline Wake-Word:** The wake-word engine ("Hey Mahesh") processes audio locally on your device hardware using a short circular buffer.
* **No Background Audio Uploads:** Audio is never uploaded to any cloud server while idling or listening for the wake word.
* **Transient Speech Recognition:** Voice commands are processed in real-time to execute device actions and are never stored or sold.

### 2. Contacts and Phone Calling
* Contact search and direct calling operate locally on your phone using Android's native `ContactsContract` and `TelecomManager`.
* Your address book is never exported or shared with third parties.

### 3. Accessibility Service
* Accessibility APIs are utilized solely for user-requested screen navigation and text entry (e.g. sending a WhatsApp message upon your command).
* We do not log keystrokes, personal messages, passwords, or financial information.

### 4. Data Sharing & Security
* We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
* All network communications use industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact: `developer@heymahesh.ai`
